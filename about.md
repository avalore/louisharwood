## Professional Background

I'm a software engineer with extensive experience across multiple platforms and domains. I enjoy the full breadth of building software — from native desktop apps to cloud infrastructure — and I'm particularly drawn to proof-of-concept work, solving unusual challenges, and shipping products that prove ideas are possible.

### Apple Platforms

I've been building macOS SDKs and frameworks for years, with deep knowledge of the platform's native tooling and APIs. I've been involved with the iOS App Store since its launch, working across various companies to ship apps to millions of users.

### Fullstack Web Development

I've designed and built CRMs and other Proptech tools for the property industry, working across the full stack with PHP, Python, Angular, and Next.js. From database design to frontend interfaces, I'm comfortable owning the entire delivery.

### Cloud & DevOps

Extensive AWS architecture experience — designing, deploying, and maintaining production infrastructure. CI/CD pipeline design and implementation to keep teams shipping reliably.

### What I Enjoy

I particularly enjoy building proof of concepts and experimental apps that aren't the norm — finding creative solutions to unique challenges and proving they're possible. If it's an interesting problem, I want to build it.
