RAGent

AI-powered property intelligence for estate agents.

RAGent uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation to help estate agents answer property queries instantly — pulling from listings, local data, and market insights to deliver accurate, contextual responses.

Features

Instant Property Q&A

Answer buyer and seller questions with AI-assisted, accurate responses grounded in your actual data.

Document Ingestion

Feed in brochures, EPC data, floorplans, and listing details. RAGent indexes it all and makes it searchable.

CRM Integration

Connects to existing estate agency CRM workflows. Fits into how you already work, not the other way around.

Built for Agents

Purpose-built for the property industry. Not a generic AI tool — designed around how estate agents actually operate.
